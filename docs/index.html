SKOPOS

AI agents stall at 90%. skopos fixes the last mile.

Your agents make assumptions. When those assumptions are wrong, the work breaks down — not at the start, but near the end, after you've invested the time. skopos gives every AI CLI on your machine a shared source of truth: synced config, fenced per-tool instructions, and a delivery loop that surfaces assumptions before they derail the work.

the doctrine

Observe. Reason.
Deliver.

The main session wears the Skopos persona: a gatekeeper that never burns context on grunt work and never delegates judgement.

/01

Observe

Scouts fan out — one per relevant repo, in parallel, each with a single targeted question. Heavy reading happens out there, not in your context window.

/02

Reason

Synthesis, trade-offs, and decisions stay in the main session. Judgement is never outsourced to a subagent.

/03

Deliver

The narrowest specialist that can do the job gets a five-line brief and returns a compressed report. No transcript recaps, ever.

the delivery loop

The last 10%
is an assumption.

Agentic delivery reaches 90% in one shot and stalls — almost never because the code was wrong, but because something was assumed and never checked. Skopos treats assumptions as the deliverable.

STAGE 1

Discover

feature-interview

One stakeholder at a time, in their own words. Async and portable — each interview reads the last, so disagreement surfaces instead of averaging away.

STAGE 2

Charter

feature-charter

Observable acceptance criteria, the assumption ledger, explicit non-goals, and the conflicts that still need a human decision.

STAGE 3

Plan

feature-plan

Scout fan-out for blast radius, then phases mapped to criteria — starting with a timeboxed spike that makes the riskiest assumption fail cheaply.

STAGE 4

Evaluate

feature-eval

Written before the build, for someone who read none of the code: exact URLs, negative scenarios, and probes designed to falsify each assumption.

STAGE 5

Retro

feature-retro

Grade every assumption, name the stage each miss was first knowable at, and graduate only the lessons that would change what happens next time.

The assumption ledger

Every assumption carries who holds it, its cheapest disproof, and the blast radius if it's wrong. The riskiest one becomes Phase 0 — a sandboxed spike, not a careful build. After ship, each is graded, and the lessons land in the project's own docs/skopos/GUIDANCE.md — read by every future session, in any AI CLI.

inverted ownership

Your files
stay yours.

engsys owned the file and fenced you in. Skopos inverts it: you own ~/.claude/CLAUDE.md — skopos owns only one fenced block inside it.

# My CLAUDE.md — mine, untouched, forever my own rules, my own notes… <!-- SKOPOS:MANAGED:START --> persona · tone · repo registry · roster ← regenerated by `skopos update` <!-- SKOPOS:USER-FACTS:START --> your durable facts — survive every update ← yours, never drift <!-- SKOPOS:USER-FACTS:END --> <!-- SKOPOS:MANAGED:END -->
yours — never touched managed — hash-verified, drift → exit 2 user facts — preserved through every regen
